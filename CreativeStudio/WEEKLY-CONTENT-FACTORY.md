# Weekly Content Factory — PARKED IDEA (not built)

**Status:** Parked Friday, June 12, 2026. Idea only. Nothing built yet. Do NOT put this on a timer until the test at the bottom passes.

## What it is
A system that drafts a full week of content for each client ahead of time, so Cal reviews and approves instead of building from a blank page every week.

## The dream
Every Sunday, before Cal sits down, a full week of content is already drafted for each client (Unik, Salvia Lion, Nail'd It). Cal opens one page, reads it, fixes anything, says yes.

## Cal's real weekly process today (the idea has to respect this)
Cal currently spends a solid day a week on content. That day is NOT just "make posts." It is:
1. Review the past week's content. How each post actually did (the numbers).
2. Decide what he liked and what he didn't. His taste, his call.
3. See what was trending per project.
4. THEN build the new week off all of that.

The review-and-taste part is the real work, and it is HIS. AI cannot have his taste. Any version of this MUST keep that step with Cal.

## The honest split: what can run on its own vs what can't
Cal's day is really two jobs stuck together.

**Job 1, grunt work (CAN be automated):**
- Pulling last week's numbers together in one place.
- Checking what is trending per project.
- Writing the rough first draft of the whole week (posts, captions, hooks, picture ideas).

This is most of the hours and it is boring.

**Job 2, judgment (STAYS Cal's):**
- What worked, what he liked, what felt off, what to cut.

This is the 20% only Cal can do.

**The win:** Job 1 is done and waiting, with last week's numbers and the trends sitting right next to it. Cal reacts to a finished draft instead of a blank page. A full day turns into about an hour.

## It gets better over time
Every week Cal says what he liked and didn't. That feedback gets written into the client's rules. So the next week's draft already leans his way. It slowly gets closer to how Cal would do it himself.

## What we already have (not starting from zero)
- **/engine skill** already runs a weekly content system for ONE account. It reads the account's own words, pulls trends, and fills a fixed machine of posts, hooks, and a roadmap. That is most of Job 1 already.
- Client folders, brand rules, voice profile, content trackers.

So the build is mostly: run /engine for ALL clients at once, add a "look back at last week" step on the front, and put it on a weekly timer.

## Honest catches and guardrails (do not skip)
- **Cal approves everything. No auto-posting.** It drafts and lines up. It never posts on its own.
- **No spending credits on auto-pilot.** Images still cost money and need Cal's eye, so image gens still wait for Cal's explicit go (matches the global credits rule).
- **Performance numbers need a source.** To pull last week's numbers automatically, the platforms have to be connected (Buffer for TikTok, Meta for IG and FB). If they are not connected, Cal shows or pastes the numbers and the rest still works.
- **It drafts, Cal judges.** The taste step never gets handed to the AI.

## The first test (do this BEFORE building any automation)
Prove the content is good before wiring up any timer.
1. Cal picks one client.
2. Cal shows last week's posts and roughly how they did.
3. Claude checks what is trending for that client and drafts the full week in Cal's style.
4. Cal says what is good and what is not. Claude saves it into that client's rules.

If the draft is genuinely good and close to Cal's taste, THEN build the all-clients plus weekly-timer version. If not, fix the draft quality first. Never automate a weak draft.
