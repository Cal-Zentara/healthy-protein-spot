# UNIK Copywriting Strategy — LOCKED June 14, 2026
*Built by applying the Sabri Suby copywriting playbook (`CreativeStudio/COPYWRITING-PLAYBOOK.md`) + the customer-voice research (`docs/facts/customer-voice-research.md`) to UNIK. This is the "how we talk and what we post" spine. Read with those two files.*

## The Spine (the one angle everything points at)
**"Buy once, done for the year."** Durability.
- From the #1 mom pain in the research: re-buying pants in November when the knees blow out. The rage isn't that pants rip, it's having to BUY THEM AGAIN.
- Lead durability; the under-$15 price is the pleasant surprise, never the lead.
- Runs the WHOLE back-to-school season (mid-June to late August). Do NOT change the spine weekly. Change only when (a) the data goes flat over several posts, or (b) the season shifts (fall = "looks sharp for picture day", or a cultural/holiday angle).
- **Condition before going all-in:** confirm with Thuy/Ben that the pants genuinely hold up. A durability claim the product can't back is worse than no claim.

## Spread the promise across the whole line (not just pants)
The promise stays narrow and sharp; the PRODUCT can be the whole catalog. Same durability promise, rotate the product:
- Pants: "Busted knees by November? Not these pants."
- Polo: "Still white in May. We checked."
- Dress: "Survived every recess all year."
Don't widen the message to "we sell uniforms" (that's generic, the coliseum-keyword trap). Apply the sharp message to more products.

## Hooks do 80% of the work
On-screen text, first second, top third, in HER words (pull from the research quote bank: "busted knees," "didn't make it to May," "you get what you pay for"). Build with craft, not flat ffmpeg boxes — use the school-paper animated hook style (see the hook-overlay engine memory).

## Repurpose: one video becomes many posts
Don't make 5 things. Make 1 and change the cut, shape, format, or hook:
1. Widescreen (16:9) → Facebook + Amazon
2. Vertical crop (9:16) → Instagram / TikTok Reel (the reach play)
3. A second clip → its own Reel (different angle, e.g. "Pick your color. They all last.")
4. A still frame → feed photo / carousel / proof static
5. 3 seconds + a poll → Story
Only the vertical recut takes real editing. Proven June 14 2026 on the BU03 pants video.

## Format jobs (each format has ONE job)
- **Reels = reach** (proven 10-25x the reach of statics; first 13 shares came from a reel). The spine lives here.
- **Static + "14,000+ reviews, 4.2 stars" = convert.** Cheap, proof closes moms (True Classic play).
- **Carousel = saves.** Weak for UNIK (6 reach, 0 saves). Max 1/week, slide 1 must stop a scroll alone.
- **Stories = keep current followers warm.** 3-7/day, polls + behind-the-scenes. Not a reach tool.

## Posting reality (researched June 14, 2026 — sources Hootsuite/Buffer/Sprout/CreatorFlow 2025-26, MEDIUM reliability, marketing blogs not Instagram's own data)
- A widescreen FEED post + a vertical REEL the same day do NOT compete — separate ranking surfaces. **Post both.**
- No real penalty for posting twice a day. Mild attention-split only for two of the SAME format same day.
- Small-account sweet spot: 3-5 strong posts/week + light daily Stories. Quality over volume.
- Pros' apparent "volume" is mostly Stories + repurposing, not 5 fresh posts. What to steal: batching, repurposing, and optimizing for engagement rate over follower count (half of UNIK's followers are wholesale buyers who won't engage — don't panic over low %).

## The lessons (so future sessions get it)
- You're not writing, you're listening: say her words back to her. The research is the hard part; it's done.
- Talk about HER, not UNIK (80/20). "20 years family owned" is about us — use it sparingly and only after she feels seen.
- One spine, ridden for a season. Different hooks underneath it weekly, never a new spine each week.
- Promise narrow, product wide.
- One video → many posts. One job per format.
